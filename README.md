# sales-dashboard

Horizon Dashboard

1. Project Title

📈 Sales Horizon Analytics: Sales Dashboard & 15-Day Forecast

A Power BI dashboard that shows how a retail business is performing and predicts sales for the next 15 days.

2. What This Project Does

This dashboard helps track sales, profit, and orders across different regions, categories, and time periods. It also predicts what sales might look like in the near future, so businesses can plan ahead instead of just looking backward.

3. Tools Used
Power BI Desktop – used to build the dashboard and visuals
Power Query – used to clean and prepare the raw data
DAX – used to write formulas for things like total sales, growth %, and averages
Data Modeling – connected different tables (sales, dates, products, regions) so they work together properly
Forecasting – used Power BI's built-in forecasting to predict sales for the next 15 days

4. Data Used

Source: SuperStore Sales Dataset (a public retail sales dataset)

It includes order details like date, sales amount, profit, quantity, product category, customer segment, region, state, and shipping info, covering a few years of sales data.

5. What's Inside the Dashboard

The Problem Businesses often have sales data spread across regions and categories, but no easy way to see it all in one place — and usually no idea what's coming next.
The Goal Bring all the sales data into one clear dashboard, and use forecasting to give a simple idea of what sales might look like in the next 15 days.

What You'll See

Overview Page – Key numbers (Total Sales, Units Sold, Profit, Avg. Ship Days), plus breakdowns by region, segment, and payment type
Trend Charts – Sales and profit compared month by month and year over year
Map View – Sales and profit shown by state
Forecast Page – A 15-day sales forecast, along with top-performing states
